# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Crypto-native capital allocators holding idle stablecoins on Robinhood Chain — DeFi power users, small treasuries, and operators who already understand lending, funding and basis but do not want to hand-assemble a position across four venues every week. They arrive skeptical rather than curious: the job to be done is "show me what this actually earns after every cost, tell me what breaks it, then let my wallet decide." Sessions are short and deliberate — check the scanner, inspect one recipe, approve or decline one action. A secondary audience of developers consumes the same estimates through the API.

## Product Purpose

SAJI turns fragmented onchain inputs into one measured carry strategy. It scans lending, spot and hedge venues, assembles candidate recipes, simulates net carry after every cost, validates each proposed action against a deterministic policy engine, and prepares a transaction the user signs themselves. It never takes custody and never moves funds outside a mandate. Success is a user who understands what they are signing before they sign it, and an agent that pauses itself the moment the recipe leaves its bounds.

## Positioning

The only yield agent that shows its inputs: every estimate exposes its ingredients, its costs, its timestamp and what would break it, before the wallet is ever asked to sign.

## Brand Personality

Heavy, exact, relentless. The voice is an industrial instrument panel, not a trading terminal cosplay — technical without jargon theatre, confident without ever promising a return. Copy states measurements and consequences: "execution paused", "requires your approval", "estimate only". The product should evoke the calm of a machine operating within spec, and unmistakable clarity at the moment something goes out of spec.

## Anti-references

Generic dark fintech (navy gradients, glass cards, glowing hero metric). Yield-farm dashboards that lead with an unqualified APY number. Anything that renders an estimate as a promise, hides costs behind a single headline figure, or asks for a signature before showing exactly what the transaction does. No decorative motion, no floating coins, no particles.

## Design Principles

**Every output shows its inputs.** No number appears without its ingredients, its costs and its data timestamp reachable in the same view.

**The machine is the identity.** Physical foundry materials — blackened steel, machined panels, one chartreuse signal — carry the brand. Chrome does not decorate; it indicates.

**The agent reasons, the policy engine decides.** Recommendations and executable actions are visually and structurally separate. Anything that can move funds passes deterministic validation and explicit approval.

**Stopping is a first-class state.** Paused, stale, breached and revoked are designed with the same care as the happy path, and are always reachable in one action.

**Estimates are labelled as estimates.** Live, delayed and demo data are distinguishable at a glance, everywhere the number appears.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 and large text ≥3:1 against the darkest video frame, which means every text surface sits on its own scrim rather than raw media. Full keyboard operability including the transaction-preview dialog and the emergency stop. Status changes announced through live regions, never colour alone — every state carries a label as well as a hue. `prefers-reduced-motion` receives posters instead of loops with no loss of content, and the entire product remains usable if video fails to load.
